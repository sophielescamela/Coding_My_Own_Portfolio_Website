Hello! I'm Sophie :)

I'm an illustrator and animator based in the US. I am currently a senior at the University of Wisconsin Whitewater majoring in Media Arts and Game Development. Something about me is that I make all of my work by hand! I love doing everything the traditional way, as I feel it is a more intimate experience between me and my art. It is my hope that others who are looking at my work can see how much love was poured into the making of it.

Some of my professional work includes the art assets of Dicey Diner, a roguelite deckbuilding videogame that is currently in the works to eventually be released on the PC. I do a bit of every medium because I believe that confining yourself to one is very limiting and I have a genuine love for everything that the art world has to offer.
